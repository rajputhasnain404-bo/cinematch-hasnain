How your matches are made

Hybrid recommender: 45% content-based (TF-IDF over genres, tags, director & cast — movies like the ones you loved), 45% collaborative (item-item similarity from 100,836 real MovieLens ratings — people who liked your favourites also liked these), 10% popularity (Bayesian average rating). Match % is the blended score, scaled 55–98. Full numbers on the Analytics tab.

How users rate (100,836 ratings)

Model evaluation — hold-out test

Reading the numbers: Precision@10 = share of the top 10 picks that were actually liked (held-out rating ≥ 4). Recall@10 = share of all liked held-out movies found in the top 10. RMSE = average rating-prediction error in stars (lower is better). The hybrid wins on recall — it finds more of what you like — while pure collaborative filtering is the most accurate at predicting exact star ratings.
